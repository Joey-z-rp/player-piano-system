Solenoid heat

The controller estimates each solenoid's temperature from what it's driven with. At the limit a piece pauses, and Play waits until it has cooled to 80% of it. A coil with any of its numbers at 0 isn't watched: measure its resistance, then hold it at a steady duty and record its temperature for 15-20 minutes for the other two.
